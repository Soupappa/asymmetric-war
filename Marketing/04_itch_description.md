## Description courte (above the fold)

```
A real-time idle war simulation built around one historical truth: technologically dominant powers frequently lose asymmetric conflicts — not through military failure, but through economic exhaustion and political collapse.

You can play both sides. Or watch them destroy each other.
```

## Description complète

```
**Two forces. Two completely different games.**

**The Coalition** has jets, Patriot batteries, satellite scans, and a GDP to match. What it doesn't have is time. Every week of war adds debt. Debt erodes the Economy index. Economy × Public Support = Hand Free — the only number that matters. When Hand Free hits zero, the Coalition withdraws. It can win every battle and still lose the war.

**The Régime** has drones, buried bases, and patience. It doesn't need to win. It needs to survive. Build bases. Bury them three levels deep. Disguise some as civilian installations. Build fake ones to waste precision strikes. Wait for the Coalition to run out of political will. If Resilience reaches the threshold — the nuclear program unlocks.

**The combat log doesn't pull its punches.**

> "A $20,000 drone just absorbed a $2,000,000 Patriot intercept. Coalition calls it a defensive win. The spreadsheet has other thoughts."

> "Civilian base struck without scan confirmation. International reaction: immediate. Régime Fervor: +12. Coalition Public Support: −18. Worth it?"

**Features:**

- 4 play modes: Coalition only, Régime only, Both sides, Observer (AI vs AI)
- 2 doctrine trees per faction — Spin Doctor vs War Falcon for the Coalition, Supreme Leader vs Tactical Harassment for the Régime
- 6 scenarios with distinct strategic profiles: standard asymmetry, nuclear race, economic warfare, iron storm, and more
- Real-time satirical combat log — milestone events, ironic cost commentary, post-game doctrine report
- Full AI for both sides — watch the Persistent Hydra absorb strikes while the Central Banker issues bonds to fund its own collapse
- Runs in the browser — no install

**This is a game about how wars self-sustain.** About why the strong side keeps spending after it should have stopped. About the gap between military reality and political reality. About the absurd economics of interception.

It's also just a very good idle strategy game.

→ Play now: [URL]
```
